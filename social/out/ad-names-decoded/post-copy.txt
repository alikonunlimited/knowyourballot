"Paid for by..." tells you the group's name — not who's funding it. 💸

We decoded 5 groups running ads in Ohio's U.S. Senate race (Sherrod Brown and Jon Husted), from both sides:

• Ohio Flyer PAC: A super PAC set up to back Jon Husted. Its top donor ($1M) is a dark-money group that doesn’t disclose its own donors.
• WinSenate: Backs Sherrod Brown. An arm of Senate Majority PAC; its biggest funder is Majority Forward, a dark-money group.
• No Going Back PAC: Spent $20M+ against Brown. Its filings listed no donors as of mid-October; the NYT and Reuters report it’s funded by Trump’s super PAC, MAGA Inc.
• Buckeye Freedom PAC: Spent about $1.6M attacking Husted and promoting the Libertarian candidate. Donors disclosed so far: none.
• Enough Promises Ohio: Its mailers attack Brown and promote an independent. Its only donor Oct. 1–14: a Republican super PAC ($2.5M). The independent says he has no ties to it.

"Dark money" groups are nonprofits that don't have to name their donors. Every group, every donor we could verify, with sources: https://knowyourballot.netlify.app
