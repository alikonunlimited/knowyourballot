KNOWYOURBALLOT — "AD TRANSLATOR" SOCIAL VIDEOS
=================================================

Each folder is one ready-to-post video (30–45 seconds, vertical 1080x1920, sound effects included):

  senate-ads-translated/     Ohio Senate ads: what the ad says vs. what the record says (4 claims, both sides)
  governor-ads-translated/   Ohio governor ads, same format (4 claims, both sides)
  ad-names-decoded/          "Paid for by..." group names vs. who actually funds them (5 groups)

In every folder:
  <name>.mp4                         the finished video (text is already on screen)
  thumbnail-vertical-1080x1920.jpg   cover for Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels
  thumbnail-square-1080x1080.jpg     Instagram grid / Facebook feed
  thumbnail-landscape-1200x675.jpg   X (Twitter) and Facebook link posts
  post-copy.txt                      captions for Facebook, Instagram and X, plus the on-screen text timeline
  captions.srt                       closed captions (same text that's on screen)

HOW TO POST
-----------
Instagram (Reel)
  1. Open Instagram, tap +, then Reel, and pick the .mp4.
  2. Optional: tap the music icon to add trending audio, then lower "Original audio" to about 30%.
  3. Tap Next, then Edit cover, then "Add from camera roll", and choose thumbnail-vertical-1080x1920.jpg.
  4. Paste the INSTAGRAM caption from post-copy.txt. Make sure your bio link points to knowyourballot.netlify.app.
  5. Share. Also share it to your Facebook page if they're connected.

Facebook (Reel or page post)
  1. On your page, choose Reel (or Photo/video) and upload the .mp4.
  2. Paste the FACEBOOK text from post-copy.txt. Links are clickable here.
  3. Set the cover to the vertical thumbnail. For a regular video post, upload captions.srt under Captions.
  4. Publish.

X / Twitter (same platform)
  1. Compose a post and attach the .mp4.
  2. Paste the X text from post-copy.txt. It's already under 280 characters.
  3. Post. Optional: reply to your own post with the landscape thumbnail and the site link.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Posting these normally is fine. If you ever pay to BOOST one on Facebook or Instagram, Meta requires
    ad-authorization (ID check) and a "Paid for by KnowYourBallot" disclaimer for political ads.
  - No campaign footage is used, so there are no copyright claims on the video itself.
  - Facts come from the site's fact-check and funding data. The jokes only poke fun at how ads look and
    what group names sound like, never at a candidate personally.
